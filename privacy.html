Trans Privacy Policy

Effective 16 July 2026. This policy describes the Trans mobile and desktop application operated by Joseph Licht (“Trans”, “we”, or “the Service Provider”).

Data Trans processes

Trans can process account information (including email address, username, user id, avatar and authentication data), app settings, searches, journeys, favorite places, friend relationships, blocks, messages, tickets, reports, and device location. Route and station requests are sent to the transport provider selected in the app and can contain station identifiers, search text, coordinates, travel times, and routing preferences.

Location is used for current-location route planning, nearby transit, wake alarms, automatic journey detection, and optional friend sharing. Trans does not intentionally build a historical trail for Privacy Level: the cloud presence record contains the latest location snapshot and replaces it when updated.

Privacy Level friend sharing

Privacy Level is available only between accepted friends. A global level applies by default and can be replaced by a per-friend override. The effective level controls what that friend receives:

LevelInformation available to the friend
0Nothing (Ghost).
1Current or recently used transit line.
2Journey start and end times.
3Destination station.
4Transit stations, lines and transfers; walking addresses are excluded.
5Progress along the detected journey without exact coordinates.
6Exact location while the detected journey is active.
7Latest exact location, usable as a From/To routing snapshot.
8Synced favorite place labels and locations.

Higher levels include lower levels. Route searches remain private until Trans has enough time and location evidence to identify a candidate as the journey currently being taken. Detection can be wrong; the app provides a “Not this journey” action. Setting the global level to 0 clears published presence; a per-friend level 0 hides it from that friend. Removing or blocking a friend removes their authorization.

Location operation and battery use

Journey detection normally monitors only around the departure of an open candidate and during a detected journey. It uses distance filters and reuses an existing wake-alarm or always-available location stream where possible. Levels 7–8 can operate in the background with a low-accuracy, distance-filtered stream and may cause the operating system to display a persistent location indicator. Location sharing cannot operate when the required system permission or location service is unavailable.

Storage, recipients, and retention

Account and social data is stored using Supabase. Authorized friends receive only the Privacy Level fields allowed by the effective sharing level. Transport providers receive information necessary to answer transit requests. Device and platform providers may process permission, notification, and network information as part of operating the device. Trans does not include a dedicated advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

Recent and frequent searches are limited in the app, saved journeys expire after arrival plus 24 hours, recent line status expires, and published journey status expires after the journey. A latest level-7 location can remain until it is replaced, sharing is set to level 0, or the account is deleted. Messages, account settings, favorites, friendships, and other account data remain while needed to provide the account or until deleted, subject to technical backups and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can lower the global level, set a lower override for a friend, use level 0, remove or block a friend, deny operating-system location permission, clear supported local history, or delete your account. Uninstalling stops future collection by that installation but does not by itself delete cloud account data.

Security

Trans uses authenticated access and database authorization rules to limit account and friend data. No system can guarantee absolute security. A friend who is authorized to view information can record or share what they see, so use levels 6–8 only with people you trust.

Children and changes

Trans is not intended to knowingly solicit personal data from children. Where local law requires parental consent, a parent or guardian must provide it. We may update this policy when the app changes and will publish the updated effective date here.
